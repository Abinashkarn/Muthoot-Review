What a record contains

✓Every pouch: label, description typed at the door, in/out times, vault weight

✓Record hash and the audit-chain position; export is signed

What is logged

Sign-ins, every request/approve/decline with the confirm-step state, every unlock command and the box's acknowledgement, lid and lock events, pouch add/remove/receive, customer actions (verify, approve, confirm, stop), overrides with both admins and the reason, settings changes with before/after, user and role changes, exports.

What is not

Admin override

The last resort when the normal keys cannot work — a box stuck sealed with a failed approver route, a customer who stopped by mistake and cannot be reached, a device fault. Two admins, a written reason, logged forever, reported to HO the same day.

What an override is not

Not a shortcut when an approver is slow (use the backup), not a way to open at a door (the customer is the key there), not available to Ops or view-only. Three overrides in a month at one branch trigger an HO review.
