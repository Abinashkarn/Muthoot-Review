Muthoot Gold Pickup · Final UI

Four surfaces of one product, built on the Track0tic design system. Open a surface, pick a screen from the top bar or press the buttons to walk the flow. On the three phone surfaces, tap English / हिन्दी to switch every screen in place. No login needed.

Customer's phone · 15 screensCustomer

Confirms the appointment the day before; at the door scans the box, verifies with an OTP on their own phone, approves the opening, confirms the pouch list before the seal, gets a receipt and a 30-day record link.

Open → Collector's phone · 25 screensCollector

Logs in, scans the box, goes to the customer, waits for the customer's approval, adds pouches, seals, drives to the branch, waits for the approver, hands pouches to the custodian. Plus No Pickup Today and every exception.

Open → Branch approver's phone · 9 screensBranch approver

The second key at the branch and the fallback at the door. Sees today's requests, watches customer-approved unlocks, approves with a confirm step, declines with a reason, gets alerts.

Open → Web portal · 20 pagesPortal

Live dashboard, pending requests, alerts, pickups and work orders, boxes, vault receipt, pouch records, people, users & roles, onboarding, reports, audit log, override, settings.
